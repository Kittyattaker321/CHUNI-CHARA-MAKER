# Chuni Chara Maker

Put your own characters into CHUNITHM, using any picture you like.
No image editor and no editing game files by hand.

> Free, unofficial fan-made tool. Not made or supported by SEGA. Contains no game files.

## What it can do

- **Add new characters** with your own pictures
- **Replace** one of the game's characters (delete yours later and the original comes back)
- **Transformations**: up to 9 extra looks per character
- **Your own group and version label** for your characters
- **Remove plain backgrounds** from your picture
- **Check a folder** for mistakes and fix them

The game's own files are never changed. Everything goes into your own option folder.

## Get started

1. Install **[Python 3](https://www.python.org/downloads/)**. Tick **"Add python.exe to PATH"** in the installer.
2. Download **ChuniCharaMaker-v1.0.zip** from [Releases](../../releases) and unzip it (right-click → *Extract All*).
   Keep the folder **outside** your game's `bin\option` folder.
3. Double-click **`Start Chuni Chara Maker.bat`**.
   The first time, it installs two small add-ons (needs the internet once).
   Keep the black window open while you use the app.

## Make a character

1. Pick your game folder and make your **option folder** (the app walks you through it).
2. Click **+ New character**, then **Open image…**
3. Move the three coloured boxes to choose what the game shows:
   - **Blue**: the whole character
   - **Orange**: head to knees
   - **Green**: the face
4. Type a **name**.
5. Click the yellow **Add** button, then start the game. Your character is already unlocked.

**For step-by-step pictures, open `Guide.html`.**

## Good to know

- **Undo everything:** delete your option folder inside `bin\option`.
- **Keep your characters safe:** the game reads option folders A→Z, and later ones win.
  Use **Make it load last** when you make a folder, so other packs can't overwrite yours.
- **Something not showing up?** Use **Check a folder…** then **Fix**.
- Only use art you made or have permission to use, and credit the artist.
