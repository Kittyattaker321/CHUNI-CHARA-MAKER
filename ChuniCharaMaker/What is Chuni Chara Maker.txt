WHAT IS CHUNI CHARA MAKER?
==========================

Chuni Chara Maker lets you put your own characters into CHUNITHM, using any
picture you like. No image editors, no editing game files by hand.


WHAT IT DOES, IN SHORT
  1. You open a picture of your character (a PNG with a see-through background
     works best).
  2. You move three boxes over the picture to choose what the game shows:
       - the big picture (shown on the result screen and when you pick a form),
       - the select picture (shown on the character select screen),
       - the small face icon (shown on your nameplate).
  3. You type a name, pick a group and a version label, and click Save.
  4. The app turns your picture into the game's own picture format and writes
     everything the game needs into your own "option folder".
  5. Start the game: your character is there, already unlocked.


WHAT ELSE IT CAN DO
  * Transformations: give a character up to 9 extra looks, each unlocked at a
    character rank you choose.
  * Replace: swap one of the game's characters for yours. Delete yours later and
    the original comes back.
  * Groups and version labels: sort your characters into your own group, with
    your own label above it.
  * Option folders: keep different sets of characters in different folders.
  * Remove background: cut a plain background away from your picture.
  * Check a folder: find and fix common mistakes in any option folder, including
    ones you downloaded.


IS IT SAFE FOR MY GAME?
  The app never changes the game's own files. Everything it makes goes into your
  own option folder inside bin\option. To undo everything, delete that folder.


WHAT YOU NEED
  * Windows
  * Your own copy of the game
  * Python 3 (free from python.org). Then double-click "Start Chuni Chara Maker.bat";
    the first time, it installs the two small add-ons it needs by itself.


WHERE TO LEARN MORE

Chuni Chara Maker is a free, unofficial fan-made tool. It is not made or supported
by SEGA, and it contains no game files, artwork or music from the game.
