Chuni Chara Maker Guide

Put your own character into CHUNITHM in five easy steps. No image editor and no file editing needed.

Before you start

You need three things.

  • Your CHUNITHM game folder – the folder that has data and bin inside it.
  • A picture of your character. A PNG with a see-through background works best. Bigger is better: at least 1080 pixels tall.
  • Python 3 – free from python.org. When you install it, tick "Add python.exe to PATH". You only do this once.
Your game is safeThe app never changes the game's own files. Everything you make goes into your own folder. Delete that folder and everything is back to normal.

Start the app

  1. Unzip the download: right-click the zip → Extract All…. Don't run it from inside the zip.
  2. Put the ChuniCharaMaker folder anywhere you like, for example your Desktop. Not inside the game's bin\option folder.
  3. Double-click Start Chuni Chara Maker.bat.

A black window opens first. The very first time, it spends a minute installing two small add-ons the app needs (Pillow and NumPy), which needs the internet once. Then the app opens.

Leave the black window openIt stays open while you use the app. Closing it closes the app too. It closes by itself when you close the app.
TipMake a shortcut: right-click the .bat → Send to → Desktop (create shortcut).

The window at a glance

The window has five parts. You go through them from left to right.

  • 1
    Character list. Your characters (My folder) or all the game's characters (All (to replace)).
  • 2
    Your picture. Open a picture here and move the three coloured boxes.
  • 3
    Preview. Exactly what the game will show.
  • 4
    Details. Name, group and version label.
  • 5
    Save button. Puts the character into the game.

1Make your option folder

Your characters live in your own "option folder". You only do this once.

  1. The first time you open the app, a welcome window appears.
  2. Click Browse… and pick your game folder.
  3. Give your folder a name: A + 3 letters, like AZMI.
  4. See a green "loads last" line? Good. See an orange note instead? Click Make it load last.
  5. Click Create my option folder.
Why "load last"?The game reads option folders from A to Z. If two folders change the same thing, the later one wins. A name that loads last (one starting with AZ) means no other folder can hide your characters.

Then pick a version label and a group

Right after that, the app asks for two names for your folder:

  • Version label – the small text shown above the group name when you get the character (for example MIKU PACK).
  • Group – the "folder" your characters are sorted into in the game (for example My Characters).

Type a name for each and click Save. Every character in this folder gets these two.

TipAlready made a label or group in another folder? Choose Use one that already exists to share it.

2Open your picture

Click + New character, then open your picture.

  1. Click Open image… and choose your picture.
  2. Does your picture have a plain white or coloured background? Click Remove background. Move the tolerance slider if bits are left over or if the character gets cut. Undo cut brings the background back.
TipA PNG that already has a see-through background (like the one in this guide) needs no cutting at all.

3Place the three boxes

The game uses three pictures of your character. Each coloured box chooses one of them.

Large 1080 (blue)
The whole character. Shown big on the result screen and when you pick a transformation.
Select 512 (orange)
Head to knees. Shown on the character select screen and while you play.
Nameplate 128 (green)
Just the face. Shown as the small icon on your nameplate.

How to move them

  • Move: drag the box.
  • Resize: drag the small square in the corner, or use the mouse wheel.
  • Fine-tune: arrow keys nudge the box. Hold Shift while dragging for small moves.
  • Start over: Auto-frame places all three again.

The Preview on the right updates while you move the boxes. If it looks good there, it will look good in the game.

TipClick a box (or its name under the picture) to make it the active one. The active box has the bold border and the corner handle.

5Save and play

  1. Click the yellow button: Add to AZMI (it shows your folder's name).
  2. Click Yes. A small window asks you to wait while the pictures are converted. It closes by itself.
  3. Start the game. Your character is in your group, already unlocked.
That's it!Your character now appears in My folder on the left.

Transformations optional

A transformation is an extra look for the same character, unlocked at a character rank. You can add up to 9.

  1. Click + Transformation. A new tab appears next to Base (marker 1).
  2. Click Open image… and open the picture for this look.
  3. Type its Transformation name and the Unlocks at rank number.
  4. Click Save changes.

The boxes are copied from the Base

  • A new transformation starts with its boxes exactly where you put them on the Base.
  • Moving a box on a transformation only changes that transformation.
  • Moving a box on the Base also moves it on every transformation with Move with the Base's boxes ticked (marker 2).
  • Match Base puts a transformation's boxes back exactly where the Base's are.

Change or remove a character

  1. Click My folder on the left.
  2. Click your character in the list.
  3. Click Edit this. A small "One second…" window shows while your boxes are put back. Then change what you like and click Save changes.

To delete a character, click Remove from my folder.

Replace a game character optional

Instead of adding a new character, you can put your picture over one the game already has.

  1. Click All (to replace).
  2. Pick the character you want to replace.
  3. Click Replace this one, open your picture, place the boxes, then click Replace character.
Nothing is lostThe original isn't deleted. Remove yours from My folder and the original comes back.
Official charactersCharacters from the game's own folders (A000, A001…) keep their own group and version label. Those two fields are locked.

Start another set of characters

Want a separate pack, with its own label and group? Make a new option folder.

  1. Click + New option folder at the top.
  2. The name already starts as one that loads last (like AZZZ). You can type your own (A + 3 letters).
  3. Read the note under the name:
    • Green – nothing can overwrite this folder.
    • Orange – other folders load after it. The note lists them and what they change. Click Make it load last to fix that.
  4. Pick its version label and group: make new ones, or reuse the ones from your other folder.
  5. Click Create and switch to it.
Going backType the other folder's name in the Option folder box at the top and press Enter.

Check a folder when something looks wrong

Click Check a folder… at the top. The app looks for common mistakes in any option folder (yours or one you downloaded): missing or misnamed pictures, wrong sizes, broken groups, and other folders that hide or overwrite yours. Fix repairs the safe ones for you.

Help! Something isn't right

My character doesn't show up in the game

Make sure you clicked the yellow save button and saw "is ready", then restart the game completely.

Still missing? Another option folder that loads after yours may be hiding your group. Click Check a folder…, pick your folder and click Fix. Saving a character fixes this too. To stop it for good, give your folder a name that loads last (see More folders).

Another pack overwrote my character

If two folders use the same character, the one later in the alphabet wins. Make a new option folder with a name that loads last (like AZZZ) and put your characters there.

The picture looks blurry in the game

Use a bigger picture. The large picture is 1080 × 1080, so a picture at least 1080 pixels tall looks best.

There's a white or coloured edge around my character

Use a PNG with a see-through background, or click Remove background and move the tolerance slider up a little.

I can't change the group or version label

Only the first character in your folder can change them (see step 4). Open that one with Edit this. Official characters you replace always keep their own.

How do I undo everything?

Delete your option folder inside bin\option (for example bin\option\AZMI). The game is back to how it was.

Where are my saved projects?

In %APPDATA%\ChuniCharaMaker. Paste that into the Explorer address bar to open it. That's where Edit this gets your pictures and box positions back from.
